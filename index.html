📖 ShiftHub Pro — User Guide / User Guide / Guía de Uso

English — General Use

Use ShiftHub Pro to prepare daily reports, chocolate adjustments, kitchen LP verification, ingredient adjustments, pouch adjustments, and pass off reports. Enter numbers directly. Export before clearing data.

Español — Uso General

Usa ShiftHub Pro para preparar reportes diarios, ajustes de chocolate, verificación de LP en Kitchen, ajustes de ingredientes, pouches y pass off. Escribe los números directamente. Exporta antes de limpiar los datos.

🔐 Team Lead Access / Acceso Team Lead

EN: Dashboard and Daily Report are Team Lead modules. Use the internal Team Lead access code.
ES: Dashboard y Daily Report son módulos para Team Lead. Usa el código interno autorizado de Team Lead.

🍫 Chocolate

EN: Only ENROBERS AND PIPES and BED default to 100%. Tanks, AD, Decorators and Morcos must be entered manually. Change the 100% only if production ended, return was made, or it is not really full.
ES: Solo ENROBERS AND PIPES y BED vienen en 100%. Tanks, AD, Decorators y Morcos se llenan manualmente. Cambia el 100% solo si la línea terminó producción, hubo retorno o no está realmente completo.

🍳 Kitchen LP Verification

EN: Upload Excel/CSV from IMS or NetSuite. Required columns: Item, Inventory Number, LP Number and Qty/Sum of On Hand. Enter Physical Qty, LP Match and Adjust manually. R LP is the priority.
ES: Sube Excel/CSV de IMS o NetSuite. Columnas requeridas: Item, Inventory Number, LP Number y Qty/Sum of On Hand. Escribe Physical Qty, LP Match y Adjust manualmente. El LP con R es la prioridad.

📦 Pouches

EN: Enter Completion, System Qty, Physical Qty and Scrap Reported. The tool applies scrap first, calculates Production Adjustment, then Total Adjustment and Status. Status logic: Acceptable ≤5%, Attention >5% and ≤8%, Critical >8%.
ES: Escribe Completion, Qty en Sistema, Qty en Físico y Scrap reportado. La herramienta aplica scrap primero, calcula Production Adjustment, luego Total Adjustment y Status. Lógica: Acceptable ≤5%, Attention >5% y ≤8%, Critical >8%.

🧾 Pass Off

EN: Paste IMS text to detect Item, Qty and LPN. Add entries to Adjustments, Scrap, Movements or Cycle Count, then export Word.
ES: Pega texto de IMS para detectar Item, Qty y LPN. Agrega entradas en Adjustments, Scrap, Movements o Cycle Count, y luego exporta a Word.

📤 Export / Exportar

EN: Use Word or Print/PDF for professional reports. Pouches and Kitchen print in landscape automatically.
ES: Usa Word o Print/PDF para reportes profesionales. Pouches y Kitchen se imprimen en horizontal automáticamente.
